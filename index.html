dTECT feature guides

Learning dTECT

Short guides to dTECT's main features, each with a demo video and step-by-step pictures taken from the real app. They are in a sensible order for a first read.

Each guide asks for the password you were given. You only need to type it once per browser tab.

Map and navigation

  1. 1Map BasicsMove and zoom the map, choose a basemap, switch light and dark, and make room for the map.
  2. 2Switch RegionChange which region's roads, results and figures you are looking at.
  3. 3SearchFind a road by name or segment number, or a dashcam video by its file name.

Reading the network

  1. 4Network ConditionRead the region's overall condition score, and how much of the network it covers.
  2. 5Analysis LayersShow road condition, signs, pavement defects, guardrails and a heatmap on the map.
  3. 6Road Segment CardOpen a road's card to see its photos, condition score and every finding.

Videos

  1. 7Video PlaybackPlay a dashcam video with dTECT's findings drawn on top.

Managing work

  1. 8Task ManagerFind and check the region's uploaded videos and Street View surveys.
